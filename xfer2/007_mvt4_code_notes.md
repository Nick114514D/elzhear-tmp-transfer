# 007 — Movement IV: Le Carnage — code notes

Locked script: `sandbox/007/mvt4_LOCKED.py` (lineage d1 → d3, d5 → d7 → **d26**),
commit `679289a5`. Duration 401.4 s. G minor. PCM_24, −1.0 dBFS peak.

A pure allegro. No lullaby frame. The doctrine: **radical reproduction** — every source
is forced into G minor by lattice, and the samples are conjugated rather than quoted.
1672 was an incitement before it was a murder, so the movement is a film of the rally.

## The pulse

One accelerando from 0:08 to 2:38 without stopping: a heart-thud every three seconds
contracting to the rally's 132, gaining iron at 30 % of the way and pistons at the first
slab. It hands off to a stumbling roll at 2:37 and to the hunt's full-mass arrival.

## The AH

Culp's vowel cut to 0.34 s through the pulpit chain, pulsed in steady time under the
tyrant. Half-rate before the first slab; **the slab doubles it** and opens it. From 2:16
two copies run at 0.500 s and 0.531 s and phase apart and back across twenty-two
seconds — the Reich device, and the fabric that binds the mob's boiling to the hunt.

## Consequences of the slabs

Each eruption is heard backwards first (the tutti chord reversed, swelling 1.15 s into
the impact). **Slab I** doubles the AH, inflames the orator's processing over 1.4 s,
adds a second sub-stroke to the pulse, lifts the strings an octave, and converts every
subsequent roar to A♭. **Slab II** is larger, smears the chord down two semitones under
a sub-thud, adds a second metal voice to the pulse and steps the fire up.

## The music box

Kalimba-celesta hybrid: four inharmonic partials, glass-click transient, dry. Seeded
twice in the harbour and rumour so its reign is a return. Above the killing it runs on
**its own clock**, indifferent to the machine. Two fractures act as harmonic pivots —
each announced by a long rewind sweep, braked by three decelerating strikes, inhabited
by the coming chord played backwards inside the silence, and answered by the orchestra
on the new root. Fracture I drops it an octave into E♭; **fracture II lands on A♭ major
and the box becomes a chorale**, alternating G minor and A♭ major bar by bar with the
orchestra's pincer, locked to the bar-heads, dragging with the strain, compressing to
two teeth before the cut. Texture develops by warp: wow at 0.5 Hz, a wooden knock two
octaves down, a cold-room convolution, puppet-rigid timing.
